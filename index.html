🕵️ Hotel Murder

A 3-player real-time mystery game. One Detective, one Murderer and one Innocent. The murder is completely fictional.

How to play

1. All 3 players join the same room code.
2. The game secretly assigns everyone a role.
3. The Detective leaves for 5 minutes.
4. The other two players complete their secret tasks.
5. A fictional murder happens at a specific location and time.
6. After 5 minutes, the Detective returns.
7. The Detective investigates by asking questions.
8. Everyone is allowed to lie.
9. Detective chooses who they think is the murderer.
